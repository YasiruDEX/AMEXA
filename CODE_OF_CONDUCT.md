# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AMEXA to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience. We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a private [security advisory](https://github.com/YasiruDEX/AMEXA/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.
